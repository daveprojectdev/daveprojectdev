### [ScrumGames](https://scrumgames.davidameth.dev) · in production

Twenty activities for a Scrum team, in Spanish and English: ten 10-minute icebreakers to start the meeting laughing and ten retrospectives from 10 to 60 minutes. In most of them, everyone joins from their phone or computer with a code, answers, and votes; in the retros, the top-voted topics become commitments with an owner. Each retrospective has its own 3D scene with the team in it: a wheel, a sprint trial, a medal gala, an auction house, a roller coaster, and more. No accounts: if someone loses their connection or switches devices, they pick up right where they left off.

**20 activities, 3 to 15 people in real time · Rooms are deleted automatically after 15 days · Every write validated in the database**

[![The ScrumGames home page: the question “How did the sprint go?”, a row with the icons of the 20 activities, and the field to join with a room code.](https://davidameth.dev/projects/scrumgames-menu.webp)](https://scrumgames.davidameth.dev)

`HTML` `CSS` `JavaScript` `three.js` `Supabase` `PostgreSQL` `Vercel`

[See the games](https://scrumgames.davidameth.dev)

### [Living Atlas · Panama's Biodiversity](https://biodiversidad.davidameth.dev) · in production

### A bank's network lab · tested on every change

A bank's network in containers: headquarters, a data center, two branches, two internet providers, and an ATM network. On every change, GitHub Actions spins it up, cuts links while measuring what gets lost, lets a hacker try to break in, and tears it down. The 3D city replays the latest run, packet by packet.

**75 tests on every change · Worst outage: 0.2 s · 6 of 6 attacks blocked**

[![The lab's 3D view: the bank's city from above, with headquarters, the data center, and the branches in orange, cables running along the streets, and, on top, the latest run's figures and the scenario buttons.](https://davidameth.dev/projects/lab-red-vista.webp)](https://davidameth.dev/proyectos/lab-red/)

`Containerlab` `FRRouting` `OSPF` `BGP` `iptables` `Python` `GitHub Actions` `three.js`

[See the network in 3D](https://davidameth.dev/proyectos/lab-red/) (in Spanish) · [View the code](https://github.com/daveprojectdev/lab-red-banco)
